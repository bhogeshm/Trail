**Looked at:** the beat sheet, all six seam strips (−0.2 / 0 / +0.2 s: every seam is a match cut on the same mark), the 360 px phone sheet, and frames pulled from the final MP4.

**Kept warning:** `line_unread` on "YES. EVEN THAT PIXEL." (1.16 s against a 1.27 s floor). It's a 4-word punchline, and its timing matters more than the rule.

**Creative calls**
- On-screen sentence-case lines carry the VO script, so the film works muted and needs no VO.
- No music bed: none was available offline. The sound design (foley, sub swell, bass accents, the boom) stands alone. Part E of the bible gives a 75 BPM spec for adding one.
- No portfolio footage was supplied, so the film strip shows the film's own beats. It makes no claim of client work.
- "BHOGESH! MOLAGAVALLI!" is set without exclamation marks, for the dry tone.

**Weakest shot:** the hands in 01. The vector plaster fingertips read correctly, but a sculpted or photographed hand with real displacement on the paper would lift the first second the most.

**With more time:** displacement-mapped paper deformation around the cut; a real music bed; portfolio excerpts in the strip; 48 px quiet lines for a social cut.
